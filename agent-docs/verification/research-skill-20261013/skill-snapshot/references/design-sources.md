# Design sources and adaptations

Researched on 2026-10-13. These are design precedents, not evidence that this particular skill outperforms another. The implementations below demonstrate useful workflow patterns; their project claims are not independent performance benchmarks. The instructions are an original synthesis, not copied skill text.

| Source inspected | Learning used | Adaptation in this skill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matt Pocock's [grill-me entrypoint](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/grill-me/SKILL.md) and [grilling workflow](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md) | The entrypoint currently delegates to grilling. Its interview works through dependent decisions in rounds, supplies recommendations, and distinguishes facts to investigate from user decisions. | Ask only scope-changing questions whose prerequisites are settled. Research facts ourselves. Stop at an actionable brief; omit exhaustive interrogation and a separate confirmation gate. |
| Anthropic's [multi-agent research system](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system) | Iterative broad-to-narrow searches, effort matched to complexity, explicit task boundaries, citation verification, and outcome-oriented evaluations. | Decompose questions, adapt searches to gaps, preserve evidence, and check citations separately. Do not prescribe agent counts, token budgets, or require delegation. |
| Anthropic's [synthesize-research skill](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/blob/main/product-management/skills/synthesize-research/SKILL.md) | Connect findings to evidence, confidence, implications, and unanswered questions. Triangulation and contradictions can reveal context differences. | Synthesize across sources and preserve applicability. Do not transplant interview frequency scoring, persona sections, or a fixed finding count into general web research. |
| [GPT Researcher](https://github.com/assafelovic/gpt-researcher) | Separates research from report writing; supports recursive subtopic exploration and multiple output formats. | Gather evidence before drafting and adapt depth and format to the question. Avoid hard dependencies on its API stack, marketing claims, costs, or automatically generated decorative images. |
| Cochrane's [handbook, chapter 14](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-14) | Findings should expose applicability, effect magnitude, and evidence certainty; bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, and publication bias can weaken conclusions. | Use claim-level limitations and explain disagreement. This general research skill does not claim to perform GRADE assessments or formal systematic reviews. |
| ONS [visualisation guidance](https://service-manual.ons.gov.uk/data-visualisation) and [chart elements](https://service-manual.ons.gov.uk/data-visualisation/build-specifications/chart-elements) | Choose charts by the information being communicated, use readable scales and labels, and add context through annotations instead of decoration. | Match visuals to reader questions and verify their encodings. Accessibility and portable-delivery fallbacks are additional design choices. |
| OpenAI's [Build skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills) | Clear descriptions enable discovery; instructions and supporting resources are loaded progressively; instruction-only skills are a suitable starting point. | Keep the entrypoint focused and split substantial methodology and visual guidance into linked references. No provider-specific research scripts are needed. |
| OpenAI's [Testing Agent Skills Systematically with Evals](https://developers.openai.com/blog/eval-skills) | Define observable success and evaluate actual behavior and artifacts, rather than subjective impressions. | Check ambiguity handling, source-backed answers, contradictions, and delivered visuals in isolated trials. Structural validation alone cannot establish research quality. |

## Maintenance

Recheck upstream behavior when revising these adaptations; repository paths and skill interfaces can change. Keep the research workflow provider-neutral. Improve instructions when an observed failure calls for a specific correction, rather than adding universal rules for hypothetical cases.
